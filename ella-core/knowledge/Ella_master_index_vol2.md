## 2. New folders (layers volume 1 lacks)

### 17_mind_layers — the conceptual stack of mind and what governs it
- `Layer_stack_overview.md` ★ — The nine conceptual layers of mind from physiology up to meaning (body hardware → body signals → evolved drives → subcortical affect → memory → cognition → automatic/unconscious processing → self and narrative → culture and meaning) and what each contributes
- `Layer_to_folder_map.md` ★ — Crosswalk between these nine conceptual layers, the L1–L11 engineering layers and the 00–20 folders, so the two views are never confused
- `Bottom_up_top_down_control.md` ★ — Which layer overrides which, and when: body and sensory signals push up, goals and beliefs push down, and precision weighting sets the balance
- `Stress_shifts_control.md` ★ — How high arousal and stress hand control from prefrontal planning to amygdala and habit circuits (catecholamine effects on prefrontal function; Arnsten)
- `Layer_arbitration_conflict.md` — How competing outputs from different layers are resolved: urge versus plan, habit versus goal, veto windows, inhibition and winner-take-all rules
- `Brain_state_switching.md` — Global configurations (alert, explore, exploit, rest, play, social, threat) that re-weight every layer at once, driven by neuromodulators and arousal
- `Developmental_maturation_order.md` — Layers mature at different speeds (brainstem and limbic early, prefrontal into the mid-20s), which explains why regulation differs between child and adult
- `Feedback_loops_registry.md` — Catalogue of recurring cross-layer loops (fear–avoidance, rumination–low mood, stress–memory, craving–reward, shame–withdrawal) with variables and direction of influence; complements the pairwise Interactions.md matrix
- `Worked_example_layer_trace.md` — One event traced through every layer (stubbed toe → nociceptors → spinal reflex → thalamus → cortex and insula → emotion → memory → later retelling) as an end-to-end test case
- `Layer_failure_signatures.md` — What failure of each layer looks like from outside (reflex loss, flat affect, amnesia, executive failure, derealization); feeds the evaluation suite
- `Subsumption_architecture.md` — Brooks' robotics design in which lower reflex layers keep running and can be suppressed but not deleted by higher ones; an engineering template for Ella's control stack
- `Marr_three_levels.md` — Computational, algorithmic and implementational levels of description; how to specify each Ella module without mixing levels
- `Tinbergen_four_questions.md` — Mechanism, development, function and evolution: a four-question checklist to apply to every behavior Ella shows
- `Panksepp_three_process_levels.md` — Primary (subcortical affect), secondary (learning and emotional memory) and tertiary (cognitive and reflective) processes, mapped onto the layers
- `Hughlings_Jackson_dissolution.md` ○ ⚠ — Historic principle that damage, drugs or exhaustion strip higher layers first and release lower ones; useful for failure-mode design, not a law
- `Triune_brain_model.md` ○ ⚠ — MacLean's reptilian / limbic / neocortex story: historically influential and scientifically superseded; kept as a labelled myth so it is not reinvented
- `Dual_process_models.md` ○ ⚠ — System 1 / System 2 thinking (Kahneman; Evans and Stanovich): a useful shorthand but contested as a strict two-system split

### 18_evolution_human_nature — the layer shaped over millions of years
- `Evolutionary_layer_overview.md` ★ — Human nature as the product of roughly 6–7 million years since the human–chimpanzee split and hundreds of millions of years of vertebrate brain heritage: what is innate, what is learned, and why both matter
- `Brain_evolution_timeline.md` — From early vertebrate hindbrain and limbic circuits, through mammalian neocortex, to primate and hominin brain expansion
- `Ancestral_environment_EEA.md` — The environment of evolutionary adaptedness (small mobile groups, scarcity, predators, infection, no writing): the baseline for asking what each system was built for
- `Evolutionary_mismatch.md` — Why modern conditions (refined sugar, constant social comparison, screens, night-time light) can misfire ancient systems
- `Natural_selection_adaptation.md` — Adaptations versus by-products and noise, levels of selection, and the shared vocabulary for this folder, with a warning against just-so stories
- `Emotion_adaptive_functions.md` — Which ancestral problem each emotion is thought to solve (threat → fear, contamination → disgust, cheating → anger, loss → sadness), with the evidence and the critics
- `Darwin_expression_of_emotions.md` — Darwin's 1872 principles of expression, the evolutionary case for facial displays, and the modern debate over universality (Ekman versus constructionist views)
- `Smoke_detector_principle.md` — Nesse's argument that defence systems are tuned to avoid costly misses, so frequent false alarms are expected and cheap; a calibration rule for fear and anxiety
- `Negativity_bias.md` — Why bad outweighs good in attention, memory and mood; the ancestral asymmetry between lethal and merely beneficial errors
- `Preparedness_learning.md` — Seligman's preparedness: fear of snakes and spiders is learned faster and extinguishes more slowly than fear of neutral or modern objects
- `Behavioral_immune_system.md` — Schaller: disgust and avoidance as pathogen defence, and its links to purity norms and out-group wariness
- `Kin_selection.md` — Hamilton's inclusive fitness: why care is typically stronger toward relatives
- `Reciprocal_altruism.md` — Trivers: tit-for-tat cooperation, cheater detection, and the roles of gratitude, guilt and anger as enforcement
- `Cheater_detection.md` ⚠ — Cosmides: specialised reasoning for spotting rule violators; the Wason-task evidence and the debate over domain-specificity
- `Parental_investment_theory.md` — Trivers: unequal investment shapes mate choice, jealousy and caregiving; evidence is mixed, so every claim is labelled a hypothesis
- `Mate_choice_sexual_selection.md` — Sexual selection, attraction cues, pair bonding and mate guarding; hypotheses kept labelled, stereotypes excluded
- `Attachment_evolution.md` — Bowlby's evolutionary logic: infant proximity-seeking to protectors and separation distress as a survival alarm
- `Social_brain_hypothesis.md` — Dunbar: neocortex size tracks social group size (about 150 stable relationships); debated against ecological explanations
- `Cooperative_breeding.md` — Hrdy: human infants are raised with help from many caregivers, which shapes infant social attention and empathy
- `Tribal_psychology_coalitions.md` — Coalition detection, in-group favouritism and out-group suspicion as ancestral tools
- `Life_history_strategy.md` ⚠ — Fast versus slow life-history strategies and the proposal that early adversity tunes development (Ellis, Belsky); evidence is contested
- `Language_evolution.md` — Hypotheses for the origin of language (gesture-first, vocal grooming, symbolic thought) and what FOXP2 does and does not show
- `Cumulative_culture_gene_culture.md` — Gene–culture coevolution and cumulative cultural learning; why human development depends on caregivers and teachers
- `Pain_protective_function.md` — Pain as a protective alarm; congenital insensitivity to pain shows the cost of lacking it
- `Evolutionary_psychiatry.md` — Nesse: why selection leaves vulnerability to anxiety, low mood and addiction (trade-offs, mismatch, defence systems), with cautions against adaptationist stories
- `Human_universals.md` — Donald Brown's list of traits found in every known culture (language, music, kin terms, play, facial expressions) as the empirical baseline for "human nature"
- `Nature_nurture_debate.md` — From blank slate to gene–environment interaction: what each side got right and why the question is now "how", not "which"
- `Music_origins_evolution.md` ○ — Hypotheses for why music exists: social bonding, mate display, by-product of language and emotion systems
- `Humor_evolution.md` ○ — Play-signal, social-bonding, mate-display and false-alarm accounts of humour
- `Diet_cooking_brain_expansion.md` ○ ⚠ — Expensive-tissue hypothesis and the cooking hypothesis: how food quality may have paid for a bigger brain; contested

- `Trauma_triggers_cue_generalization.md` ○ — Overgeneralization of danger cues and failed discrimination (pattern-separation deficits)
- `Avoidance_safety_behaviors.md` — How avoidance brings short-term relief but blocks extinction: the central maintenance loop of fear and anxiety disorders
